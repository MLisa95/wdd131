About Me

My name is Lisakhanya Gasa. I was born in South Africa in a small little town called King William's Town, also now known as Qonce. I now live in Mdantsane, and am looking for work. I have 3 lovely children who I love doing scripture study and show-stopping karoake with.

About Mdantsane, South Africa

Mdantsane is located between King William's Town and East London. It is the second biggest township in South Africa, the lagest being Soweto in Johannesburg. Mdantsane is populated by beautiful mountains and streams. It has a river called the Beidle Drift River, known as uGreenika by the locals.
