Our Course

Coding & Robotics

Our hands-on syllabus introduces block coding, basic JavaScript, and robot control logic. Kids will build, test, and iterate their own mini-robots to solve real-world problems.

Skills Gained

Logical thinking, problem-solving, teamwork, creativity, and digital confidence. These foundational skills prepare children for a future where technology is second nature.

Parent Involvement

Parents can stay engaged through weekly updates and our message board—send encouraging notes, celebrate progress, and stay part of your child’s tech journey.
